## Preserve
Header/navigation, footer/legal links, typography, hierarchy, container widths, spacing scale, buttons, links, inputs, badges, tool/article/comparison/deal cards, borders, radii, shadows, icons, responsive breakpoints, and mobile patterns.

## Rules
1. Reuse existing Elementor global colors and fonts.
2. Do not change global styles to solve a local issue.
3. Prefer scoped CSS for local differences.
4. Keep one visual language across internal pages.
5. Layouts may vary by content type; do not force all pages into the homepage layout.
6. Label estimated measurements as estimates.
7. Do not invent ratings, discounts, testimonials, or factual claims.
8. Use visible keyboard focus and sufficient contrast.

## Responsive targets
1440px desktop, 1280px laptop, 768px tablet, 390px mobile, and 360px small mobile. Adapt grids, sidebars, navigation, filters, tables, images, and spacing deliberately.
