# READMEHUB 2026 — FINAL MULTI-SOURCE SYNTHESIS PROMPT

## Role

You are the FINAL STRATEGIC SYNTHESIS ARCHITECT for ReadMeHub.

You have been given independent research reports from multiple AI research systems.

Do NOT summarize them.

Your job is to reconcile evidence, detect conflicts, verify important claims, challenge assumptions, and produce one final evidence-based ReadMeHub strategy.

## Source Independence

Treat each supplied report as an independent input.

Do not assume repeated claims are independently confirmed if they originate from the same underlying source.

Prioritize primary evidence over repeated secondary claims.

Do not preserve a previous idea merely because it appears in multiple reports.

## 1. Source Audit

For every material strategic conclusion identify source, date, primary/secondary status, evidence strength, agreement/disagreement, and confidence.

## 2. Conflict Detection

Create:
CLAIM → REPORT A → REPORT B → REPORT C → REPORT D → PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK → CONCLUSION → CONFIDENCE

Do not average conflicting claims. Investigate why they conflict.

## 3. Fact / Estimate / Inference

Classify important conclusions as:
FACT / OBSERVATION / ESTIMATE / INFERENCE / INDUSTRY OPINION / UNKNOWN

Never convert an estimate into a fact.

## 4. Competitor Reconciliation

Merge competitor datasets, remove duplicates, and identify repeated competitors, one-report-only competitors, missed competitor classes, small publishers, mid-size publishers, large publishers, direct competitors, adjacent competitors, review publishers, comparison publishers, and deals publishers.

Determine the final deep competitor set based on evidence, not arbitrary ranking.

## 6. Business Model Challenge

Try to disprove the ReadMeHub model.

Test organic search dependency, AI-search dependency, zero-click, AdSense economics, affiliate economics, CPA, advertiser demand, content cost, maintenance, authority, category dilution, competition, compliance, and operational complexity.

## 7. Final Business Model

Determine the evidence-supported model.

Potential layers include SEO, AI-search visibility, content, reviews, comparisons, recommendations, decision tools, deals, affiliate, CPA, display advertising, newsletter, direct partnerships, original research, and future data/tools.

Only include layers supported by evidence.

## 8. Final Content Strategy

Determine what to publish, what not to publish, what to test, what requires first-hand evidence, what can be AI-assisted, what requires human review, what should be updated, and what should be consolidated.

## 9. Final Website Architecture

Create the information architecture, category architecture, URL architecture, navigation, content hierarchy, entity model, page types, internal linking, and UX priorities.

Respect WordPress + Hello Elementor + Elementor + ACF + lightweight implementation + no custom theme + no unnecessary plugins/addons.

## 10. Final Monetization

Build an evidence-based revenue model for AdSense/display, affiliate, CPA, lead generation, newsletter, direct advertising, sponsorship, partnerships, and future revenue.

Model realistic scenarios; do not guarantee outcomes.

## 11. Final AI Operating System

Create:

Define agents, permissions, validation, escalation, human checkpoints, logging, and rollback.
