How we evaluate

Six steps between a question and a recommendation

Credibility is displayed, not claimed. Every piece carries its evidence level, sources, and the date it was last checked.

  1. Frame the decision

    Start from the reader's problem and constraints — not from a product to promote.

  2. Gather sources

    Primary documentation first. Secondary sources are cited and dated.

  3. Verify the facts

    Pricing, features, and limits are checked against the source, with a visible date.

  4. Test where justified

    Hands-on only when the environment and method can be documented.

  5. Publish with levels

    Every piece carries an evidence level and its limitations — never a bare verdict.

  6. Re-verify on schedule

    Dates are displayed; overdue items are marked “Verification due” until refreshed.
